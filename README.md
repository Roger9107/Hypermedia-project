# Project Analysis: Ryan Kelly Personal CV

## 1. Project Analysis: User Profile (User Persona)

* **Name:** Marc Soler
* **Role / Job Title:** Head of Scouting & Sports Analytics Director at FC Barcelona Bàsquet
* **Age:** 44
* **Location:** Barcelona, Catalonia

### Context of the persona
Marc leads the talent scouting and quantitative performance department for FC Barcelona Bàsquet, one of the best basketball organizations in the EuroLeague. Recently the club has been investing a lot in the data analysis department, with the young talents pursuing their dreams of playing in the NBA, making the right signings and decisions every year is key to accomplish the yearly objectives. They need young talent with insane potential due to the best of the best in the sector also going to the NBA. Also because of the importance of this project, they are looking for people who can also connect with the players in order to give a personalized approach to make the players and the team as good as possible. 

### What's he looking for?
* **Elite Junior Potential:** Identify young talent who can immediately contribute to tactical modeling and video integration while having lots of room for development.
* **Knowledge of the game:** Numbers might be a lot but not everything, having a big understandment of the game is a very important part for this workplace.
* **Hunger:** In this sector it's not enough with doing what's expected, going out of your way to learn more is a must.
* **Purpose:** It's not a job for everyone, the most important things aside the habilities is passion, something that nobody can teach.

## 2. Project Analysis: Information Architecture

The information architecture of this document is designed to maximize efficiency making sure that they see exactly what they are expecting, making the information the most important part not a fancy or overcomplicated design. The entire page is contained within a full page or background where there's two columns that divide the content.

The Left Column is made for the first quick look to display abilities and skills and show the contact in case they are interested. At the top, there is a circular profile picture so the visitor can put a face to the name right away. Below the photo, the column is split into four clear sections using  lines: Technical Skills, Analytics and Soft Skills, Languages, and Contact. Using lists to show the contents of these sections.

The Right Column contains the main story and career background. At the top, there's the candidate's full name in a large header and their job title in a subheader. Right below, there is a short personal paragraph explaining who Ryan is, his background, and his passion for sports analytics. The rest of the column is divided into two blocks: Experience and Education. Each section has a title and uses paragraphs with line breaks to show jobs, schools, and dates. In this case with just looking at the job title you know it's valuable it doesn't need a text explaining it.

---

## 3. Project Analysis: Visual Design

The entire layout is built on a 750px grid. I gave the left sidebar a total width of 270px, left a 50px space in the middle, and used 300px for the right column. I also added padding around the edges so the text doesn't touches the borders.

With color I didn't do much, I just picked a soft blue (#B8CEF6) as the background color for the left column and kept a clean white background (`#FFFFFF`) for the right column where most of the reading happens. This makes the page basic and easy to look at.

For typography, I chose the Roboto font keeping the same line, to keep it simpl, easy to read and to make the information stand out.
